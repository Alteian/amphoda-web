About the company

Amphoda brings people, work, and companies under one roof. It gives companies structure, standards, and room to grow. Brands stay independent and share the bar at the top.

Values

Amphoda’s values are practical, not slogans. They hold operating quality, plain language, and independent brands. Shared standards sit at the top.

  • Quality that holds up over time
  • Plain language and clear decisions
  • Independent brands in the group, shared standards at the top

How we work

Group companies follow Amphoda’s standards. Each keeps its own direction and name. Amphoda holds the bar and the decisions that must be shared.
